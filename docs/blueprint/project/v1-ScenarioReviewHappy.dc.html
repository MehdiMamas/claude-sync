CLAUDE.md review: happy path

Created 2026-11-26. Updated 2026-11-26.

As a group member, I want to review what others add to the group CLAUDE.md, so that my Claude never reads lines I did not accept. Click a step to open its screen. See the flow that leads here.

  1. Someone commits a CLAUDE.md change
    System: Reviewed through GET /compare between the last synced head and the new head (docs/architecture.md section 7a).
  2. Split into items (one per commit block)
    System: claude_md_review builds the items (engine.rs:1114); claude_md_pending counts them (engine.rs:1102).
  3. Auto-accept on? apply now
    System: autoAcceptClaudeMd in claude-sync.json is the group default; each device can override.
  4. Review card lists pending items
    System: review_pending and review_claude_md drive the card (engine.rs:53-55); nav shows the count.
  5. Accept or Decline each item
    System: Choices are stored in claude-md-review.json, format 2.
  6. Accepted lines enter ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md
    System: Items apply by content, not by line number.
  7. Declined lines are never pushed
    System: They stay out of what this device shares.
  8. State: Pending
    System: state pending in CLAUDE.md review item states (docs/architecture.md section 7a; claude-md-review.json format 2).
  9. State: Accepted: applied
    System: state accepted in CLAUDE.md review item states (docs/architecture.md section 7a; claude-md-review.json format 2).
  10. State: Declined: kept out
    System: state declined in CLAUDE.md review item states (docs/architecture.md section 7a; claude-md-review.json format 2).
