UML activity diagram: Background sync tick

Every interval (1 minute by default, 30 s to 15 min, as v1) the engine asks GitHub, per repo, whether main moved. Unchanged repos answer 304, which costs no rate limit. A moved repo is diffed by blob sha, a plan is made, and the plan is applied in one store transaction. Red: rate limit, offline. Created 2026-11-26. Updated 2026-11-26.

Why REST with ETags, not GraphQL
A 304 costs nothing against the primary limit; GraphQL has no conditional requests, and one path is simpler than two (ADR-003).
