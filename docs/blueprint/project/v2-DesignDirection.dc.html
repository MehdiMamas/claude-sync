Visual direction

Created 2026-11-26. Updated 2026-11-26.

Palette and type from: app/ui/src/style.css (the v1 product palette, kept so v2 looks like the same product).

Same product, same people: friends who share a Claude Code setup and open the window a few times a week. The rewrite changes structure, not the look.

Three adjectives

  • Plain: system font at 14px, no brand font.
  • Calm: grey page, white cards, one blue accent; red only for failures, amber only for waiting.
  • Honest: every state says what happened and what to do (Rate limited until 14:05), never a colour alone.

What changes from v1

  • A left nav (Groups, Settings, Feedback, Notifications) instead of stacked pages, so every screen is one click away.
  • Group sub-tabs: Overview, Review, Contents, Members, History; Settings tabs: General, Personal, Notifications, Account, Updates, Advanced (Packs and SSH open from Advanced).
  • Dialogs (leave, revert, transfer, sign out) are their own frames.

One detail only this subject has

The two linked rings logo: two machines kept in step.

impeccable detect

Ran impeccable 4.8.2 detect on all v2 frames (2026-11-26): 482 advisory findings, no default-look (AI slop) pattern. Kept on purpose, with reason:

  • Low contrast (251) is fixed: the v1 accent (4.4:1 on the grey page) and green (4.1:1) are darkened to #2862c4 (5.4:1) and #197a42 (5.0:1); the hue is unchanged.
  • Tiny or undersized text (171): 12px captions and 11px diagram labels, kept from v1 for dense lists; the build checks them at 100% zoom.
  • Cramped padding and monotonous spacing (59): compact rows are the v1 look for long lists.
