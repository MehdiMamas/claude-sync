Status: current. Created 2026-11-26. Updated 2026-11-26.

As a person with two computers, I want to see what happens when it goes wrong while I keep my own CLAUDE.md and memory in step, so that both computers act the same even then. Click a step to open its screen. See the flow that leads here.

  1. Merge + push local files
    System: personal_sync (personal.rs:466) merges and pushes; secrets are scanned first (secrets::scan, engine.rs:410).
  2. Same lines, different line endings
    System: A MEMORY.md with CRLF on one device and LF on another is never pushed or rewritten (AGENTS.md gotchas; merge_personal).
